Inspired by candlelit cafes, stone boulevards, and Paris's late-night glow, this theme brings dusky rose, plum-espresso depth, and soft blush accents into Chrome. It feels romantic, moody, and elegant without losing clarity.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Vibrant, city-inspired palette with a rich, cinematic atmosphere
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
