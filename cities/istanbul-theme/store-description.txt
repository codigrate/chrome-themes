Inspired by the soft daylight and sea breezes of Istanbul, this theme brings airy turquoise tones and warm historical accents into Chrome. It feels fresh, calm, and expressive, offering a refined browsing atmosphere with a distinct coastal elegance.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Vibrant, city-inspired palette with a rich, cinematic atmosphere
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
