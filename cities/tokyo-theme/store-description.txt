Inspired by Tokyo's neon-lit streets and midnight skyline, this theme surrounds Chrome with deep indigo tones, electric violets, and cool luminous accents. It feels sleek, atmospheric, and distinctly futuristic while staying polished.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Vibrant, city-inspired palette with a rich, cinematic atmosphere
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
