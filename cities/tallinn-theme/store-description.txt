Inspired by Tallinn's crisp light and Baltic calm, this theme pairs cool porcelain tones with Nordic blues for a browser experience that feels clean, minimal, and quietly elegant. Subtle contrast keeps everything fresh and readable.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Vibrant, city-inspired palette with a rich, cinematic atmosphere
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
