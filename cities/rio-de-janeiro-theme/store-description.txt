Inspired by Rio's lush hills, bright air, and coastal energy, this theme blends soft minty tones with vibrant greens and clean blues to create a light, refreshing Chrome experience that feels lively, open, and balanced.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Vibrant, city-inspired palette with a rich, cinematic atmosphere
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
