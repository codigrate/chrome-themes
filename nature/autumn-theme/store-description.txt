Inspired by the warm hues and rustic charm of autumn, this light theme brings soft seasonal comfort to Chrome. Earthy oranges, mellow neutrals, and crisp contrast create a cozy browsing space that feels welcoming, balanced, and quietly expressive.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Calm, nature-inspired palette designed for long, comfortable coding sessions
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
