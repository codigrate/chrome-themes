Inspired by the majestic heights and serene landscapes of Mount Everest, this light theme brings a crisp and calming presence to Chrome. Soft icy tones and clean, airy surfaces evoke snow-covered peaks and clear mountain skies, creating a browsing experience that feels fresh, focused, and easy on the eyes.

Features
- Colors the whole browser frame: toolbar, tabs, omnibox and the background surfaces
- Calm, nature-inspired palette designed for long, comfortable coding sessions
- Actively maintained and regularly refined
